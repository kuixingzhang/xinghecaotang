# Building Xinghe Cottage

# 《草堂手記》

### Notes on the Making of Xinghe Cottage

---

> *A house is built with wood and stone.*
>
> *A cottage is built with words and silence.*

---

> **草堂因文字而立，亦因留白而成。**

---

# Preface
## 序

There are books written for readers.

There are books written for builders.

This is the latter.

Building Xinghe Cottage is not a manual on web design.
It is not a handbook of typography.
Neither is it a technical document.

It is a notebook kept beside the workbench.

Here are recorded the questions, conversations, revisions, and quiet decisions that slowly shaped a place called **Xinghe Cottage**.

Some pages discuss fonts.

Some discuss margins.

Some simply preserve a thought before it fades away.

Together they tell the story of becoming.

Like the cottage itself, this notebook has no final edition.

It grows only because the cottage continues to grow.

---

世上有些書，是寫給讀者的。

也有些書，是寫給建造者的。

《草堂手記》屬於後者。

它不是一本網站製作教程。

不是一本排版規範。

更不是一份程式設計文件。

它只是放在書桌旁的一本筆記。

這裡記錄的，是《星河草堂》建立過程中的思考、討論、修改，以及那些看似微小卻改變整體氣質的決定。

有時談的是字體。

有時談的是留白。

有時只是記下一句不願遺忘的話。

它們共同記錄的，不是一個網站，而是一座草堂如何慢慢長成。

因此，《草堂手記》沒有真正的終稿。

它會和草堂一起，慢慢生長。

# Part I
# The Beginning

# 第一章　緣起

## Reflection
### 思考

Before a single page was written, one question appeared again and again.

**What kind of place should Xinghe Cottage become?**

Not merely what it should look like.

Not how many functions it should contain.

But what kind of silence should a reader feel after arriving.

Many websites begin by asking,

*"What can we add?"*

Xinghe Cottage began by asking,

*"What can we remove?"*

That single question quietly shaped almost every decision that followed.

---

在建立《星河草堂》之前，我們反覆思考的，不是網站應該長成什麼樣子。

而是：

**它應該帶給讀者一種什麼樣的感受。**

不是功能。

不是動畫。

不是配色。

而是一種閱讀開始之前的安靜。

許多網站首先思考的是：

**還可以增加什麼。**

而《星河草堂》首先思考的是：

**還可以減去什麼。**

正是這個問題，慢慢決定了後來幾乎所有設計。

---

## Decision
### 決定

The cottage would not compete for attention.

It would simply offer a place to stay.

Every element must earn its place.

If something does not help reading,

it should quietly step aside.

---

我們決定：

草堂不追求吸引目光。

它只提供一個可以停留下來閱讀的地方。

每一個元素，都必須有存在的理由。

凡是不幫助閱讀的，

便讓它安靜地退後一步。

---

## Reason
### 原因

Design should never become the loudest voice on the page.

When readers remember the interface more vividly than the writing, something has already gone wrong.

Good design does not ask to be noticed.

It allows words to remain unforgettable.

---

設計不應成為頁面上最響亮的聲音。

如果讀者記住的是介面，而不是文字，

那麼設計已經走到了文字前面。

真正好的設計，

並不要求被看見。

它只是讓文字，更容易被記住。

---
