## 孔夫子

千古誰識奎星閣，嘆鳳傷麟苦事多。  
斯文不懼奔波遠，道窮豈敢忘弦歌。  

![孔夫子](/assets/images/poetry/kongfuzi.jpg)

<h3>To Confucius</h3>

<div class="english-poem">
Who now recalls the ancient Master's hall,<br>
Where he wept to see an age's fall?<br>
Though righteousness may strive in vain,<br>
The Way grows dim, yet songs remain.<br>
</div>
