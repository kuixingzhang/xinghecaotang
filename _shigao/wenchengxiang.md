讀經明理一書生，鐵血干戈秉赤誠。  
萬里奔波南北道，寸心不變虜王庭。  
漢賊消長惟天意，中夏長安賴俊靈。  
國破身亡魂未滅，浩然正氣譜丹青。

## To Wen Tianxiang
<h3>To Wen Tianxiang</h3>

<div class="english-poem">
A scholar seeking truth alone,<br>
Yet forged a heart as hard as stone.<br>
Through north and south your footsteps ran,<br>
Yet never bowed to mortal man.<br>

The rise and fall are Heaven's decree,<br>
Yet faithful hearts preserve the free.<br>
Though kingdom fell and life was gone,<br>
Your righteous spirit still lives on.<br>
</div>
