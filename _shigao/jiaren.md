## 佳人

膚如凝脂氣如蘭，羅衿難耐五更寒。  
溫柔鄉裡名利客，但逐心浪不知還。  

![佳人](/assets/images/poetry/jiaren.jpg)

<h3>The beauty</h3>
<div class="english-poem">
Her skin was fair, her breath like bloom;<br>
Silk could not warm the night's last gloom.<br>
The seekers after wealth and fame,<br>
Were swept away by passion's flame.<br>
</div>
