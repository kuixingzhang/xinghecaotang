沒有你的日子，曾經目空一切。  
只覺世事輕易，除非自己不屑。  

沒有你的日子，曾經苦苦尋覓。  
讀遍古今聖賢，不見人生真諦。  

沒有你的日子，曾經舉步維艱。  
好像已離困境，心卻不能平安。  

沒有你的日子，曾經絕望消沉。  
活著本無指望，死也不能還魂。  

如今擁有了你，靈裡好像重生。  
一切重擔卸下，渾身自在輕鬆。  

如今擁有了你，不再苦苦掙扎。  
生命有人做主，貧窮也是通達。  

如今擁有了你，中心不再彷徨。  
活水湧如江河，喜樂無法隱藏。  

如今擁有了你，不再寂寞虛空。  
你是宇宙至寶，生活滿了溝通。

<h3>With you</h3>

<div class="english-poem">
Without You, all the world seemed mine to claim;  <br>
Nothing appeared too hard to tame.  <br>
Life looked simple, bright, and free—  <br>
Unless my own heart disagreed.<br>

Without You, I searched both far and near;  <br>
Through every voice I held most dear.  <br>
I sought the truth with all my might,  <br>
Yet never found life's guiding light.<br>

Without You, each step was hard to take;  <br>
Though outward chains began to break,  <br>
The storms within refused to cease;  <br>
My restless soul could find no peace.<br>
<br>
Without You, hope slowly slipped away;  <br>
Life had no dawn beyond the gray.  <br>
Death offered neither rest nor grace,  <br>
Only silence without a face.<br>

---

Now with You, my spirit lives anew;  <br>
Every burden fades from view.  <br>
Hands once weary, heart once worn,  <br>
Wake as though this day were born.<br>
<br>
Now with You, I cease my endless fight;  <br>
Another guides my path aright.  <br>
Whether rich or poor I stand,  <br>
Content within Your faithful hand.<br>
<br>
Now with You, my heart no longer strays;  <br>
Living waters flood my days.  <br>
Joy pours forth like rushing streams,  <br>
Brighter than my brightest dreams.<br>
<br>
Now with You, no emptiness remains;  <br>
Loneliness has lost its chains.  <br>
You, the Treasure of earth and heaven,  <br>
Fill my life with living communion.

</div>
