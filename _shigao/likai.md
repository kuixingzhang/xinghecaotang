少年時我曾質疑，為什麼不讓我家富足，  
你說，我已經讓你衣食無憂。  

小學時我曾質疑，為什麼讓我遭受烈怒，  
你說，我會讓你的靈魂堅固。  

中學時我曾質疑，為什麼苦難沒有出路，  
你說，我已經讓你萬人羨慕。  

大學時我曾質疑，為什麼敏銳卻仍孤獨，  
你說，擁抱我就在你心深處。  

成家了我曾質疑，為什麼婚姻充滿痛苦，  
你說，我已給你更大的任務。  

工作後我曾質疑，為什麼求索滿了桎梏，  
你說，幸福是有我可以傾訴。

<h3>You Were Always Theres</h3>

<div class="english-poem">

In childhood I would often ask,<br>
Why was my home not rich with more?<br>
You answered, "What you truly need,<br>
I have already placed before."<br>
<br>
In school I often asked again,<br>
Why must I bear another's rage?<br>
You answered, "Through the fiercest fire,<br>
Your soul grows stronger with each stage."<br>
<br>
In youth I often asked again,<br>
Why suffering seemed without an end?<br>
You answered, "Though you cannot see,<br>
I've blessed you more than you comprehend."<br>
<br>
In college I would ask once more,<br>
Why insight walked with lonely days?<br>
You answered, "Deep within your heart,<br>
My quiet presence always stays."<br>
<br>
When I built a home, I asked,<br>
Why love should carry pain as well.<br>
You answered, "Greater work awaits,<br>
A higher calling you will tell."<br>
<br>
When work became my daily road,<br>
I asked why every step was bound.<br>
You answered, "True happiness is this:<br>
In Me, a listening heart is found."<br>
<br>
Only now I turn and see,<br>
Through every joy and every scar;<br>
Before I ever called Your name,<br>
You were always where You are.<br>
