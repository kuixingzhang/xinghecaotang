人生何患無春風，情到深處自動容。  
知音總不費思慮，會心原是任我行。  

![相知](/assets/images/poetry/xiangzhi.jpg)

<h3>Kindred Spirits</h3>
<div class="english-poem">

Why fear the loss of springtime's breeze?<br>
True hearts unfold with effortless ease.<br>
Kindred souls need little to explain;<br>
One knowing smile sets all things plain.<br>
</div>
