<h1 class="essay-title">母親</h1>

<p class="essay-subtitle">
<em>My Mother</em>
</p>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="母親一直到八十歲都體格健壯，性格開朗。八十歲那年，她接受了腦部良性腫瘤切除手術，本以為只是人生中的一次普通手術，卻沒有想到，接踵而來的是新冠疫情、長時間臥床，以及一連串難以預料的打擊。因為封城，兩個姐姐無法進家照顧她；而我遠在美國，也無法回國探望。原本熱鬧的一家人，只能隔著電話和視頻彼此掛念。"
  en="My mother remained remarkably healthy and cheerful well into her eighties. When she underwent surgery to remove a benign brain tumor at the age of eighty, we believed it would simply be another obstacle from which she would recover. Instead, it marked the beginning of a long season of trials. The COVID-19 pandemic soon swept across the country, lockdowns kept my two sisters from caring for her, and I, living thousands of miles away in the United States, could do nothing but watch from afar. A family once accustomed to gathering around her could now meet only through phone calls and video screens."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="長期臥床、腫瘤對垂體的影響，以及感染新冠，使母親出現嚴重的激素失調，一度命懸一線。所幸最終查明病因，開始接受替代治療，她也慢慢甦醒過來。我們都以為最困難的日子已經過去，然而去年父親的離世，又給了她沉重的一擊。此後，她的身體每況愈下；膽囊結石與胰腺炎使她無法正常進食，最終因長期營養匱乏，在一個寂靜的深夜，安詳地離開了這個世界。她沒有等到我回家，而這也成了我一生最深的遺憾。"
  en="Prolonged bed rest, complications involving her pituitary gland, and a severe COVID infection eventually left her with a life-threatening hormonal imbalance. After the cause was finally identified and hormone replacement therapy began, she slowly recovered, and we believed the worst had passed. But my father's death the following year dealt her a sorrow from which she never truly recovered. Gallstones and pancreatitis gradually robbed her of the ability to eat, leaving her increasingly frail. One quiet night, before I was able to return home, she passed away peacefully in her sleep. That I was not beside her in her final days remains one of the deepest sorrows of my life."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="外公三十六歲便去世了。那一年，母親只有十六歲，卻成了家裡唯一的勞動力，一面照顧年幼的弟弟妹妹，一面撐起整個家庭。家裡孩子多，生活清苦，學校又離得很遠，因此母親斷斷續續只讀過幾年書。然而，她天資聰穎，過目不忘，過耳能誦，又明事理、善言談。街坊鄰居喜歡聽她講故事，我們兄弟姐妹喜歡聽她講故事，後來連孫輩也圍坐在她身旁，聽她一遍又一遍講那些熟悉的往事。"
  en="My grandfather died at the age of thirty-six, leaving behind a family that suddenly depended upon my sixteen-year-old mother. Still little more than a girl herself, she became the family's only dependable worker while caring for her younger brothers and sisters. Poverty, a large household, and the long distance to school meant that her formal education ended after only a few years. Yet she possessed a remarkable mind. What she read she rarely forgot; what she heard she could often recite from memory. She spoke with warmth, wisdom, and uncommon clarity. Neighbors loved listening to her stories, so did my brothers, sisters, and I. In later years, her grandchildren would gather around her in much the same way, eager to hear the familiar stories that never seemed to grow old."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="母親一生待人寬厚。她孝敬長輩，疼愛晚輩，從未對人惡言相向，更沒有打罵過孩子。在我的記憶裡，她總是溫和而有耐心，無論面對家人還是鄰里，都願意體諒別人的難處。也正因如此，我們家年年都被評為五好家庭。後來我才明白，真正維繫一個家庭的，並不只是勤勞，更是一個人的品格。母親沒有讀過多少書，卻用自己的一生，教會了我們什麼是善良、忍耐與愛。"
  en="Kindness became the quiet strength of my mother's life. She honored those older than herself and loved those younger with unfailing tenderness. I never remember hearing her speak harshly to anyone, much less strike or scold a child. Whether among family members or neighbors, she instinctively sought understanding rather than conflict. Our home was recognized year after year as a model family, but I have come to believe that its greatest strength was never material prosperity. It was my mother's character. Though she had received little formal education, she taught us through her own life what kindness, patience, and love truly look like."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="早年父親在外地教書，薪水微薄，真正支撐整個家的，是母親日復一日的勞動。無論田裡的農活，還是家中的各種手藝，她幾乎一學就會。因此，即使在最困難的年代，我們家也沒有真正挨過餓。後來生活漸漸改善，她依然停不下來，不僅幫助子女操持家務、分擔勞動，又一手帶大了一個又一個孫輩。許多孩子平日吃飯、睡覺都在她家，她的家，也成了幾代人共同成長的地方。"
  en="In those early years, my father taught at a school far from home for a modest salary. It was my mother's labor, quiet and unceasing, that truly sustained our family. Whether working in the fields or mastering the countless practical skills required to keep a household running, she seemed able to learn almost anything with remarkable ease. Even during the hardest years, we never truly went hungry. As life gradually became more comfortable, she did not slow down. She continued helping her children build their own families, and in time she lovingly raised one grandchild after another. Many of them ate their meals and slept under her roof, so that her home became a place where several generations were nurtured by the same patient hands."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="如今再回首，我才漸漸明白，母親一生幾乎沒有為自己而活。她沒有遠行，沒有追求名利，也沒有留下任何驚天動地的事業；然而，她把自己的青春、勞力和歲月，都默默化作了一個家庭的溫暖。許多真正重要的生命，並不是因為站在聚光燈下而被人記住，而是因為在漫長歲月裡，默默成全了別人的人生。母親正是這樣的一個人。"
  en="Looking back now, I realize that my mother lived very little for herself. She never sought fame or recognition, nor did she leave behind achievements that history would record. Instead, she quietly transformed her youth, her strength, and the ordinary years of her life into the warmth of a home. Some lives become memorable because they stand in the spotlight. Others change the world almost unnoticed, simply by giving themselves away day after day. My mother belonged to the latter. Whatever good has grown from our family bears, in one way or another, the imprint of her faithful and selfless love."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="母親的一生，也伴隨著許多苦難。小時候，她患了中耳炎，因家境貧寒，無力求醫，最終留下了終身的聽力損傷。中年以後，她常常頭痛，我們只當是操勞過度，誰也沒有想到，真正的原因竟是一顆緩慢生長了許多年的腦膜瘤。直到八十歲做腦部電腦斷層檢查時，才發現它已經壓迫了視神經和垂體。"
  en="Suffering, too, quietly accompanied much of my mother's life. As a child she developed a severe ear infection, but poverty placed medical treatment beyond the family's reach, leaving her with permanent hearing loss. In middle age she often complained of headaches. We assumed they were simply the consequence of years of hard work, never imagining that a slowly growing meningioma had been silently pressing against her optic nerve and pituitary gland for many years. Only when she underwent a CT scan at the age of eighty did the hidden cause finally come to light."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="手術雖然成功切除了腫瘤，右眼卻因此完全失明；左眼後來也一度幾乎看不見，幸而證實只是白內障，手術後恢復了視力。激素替代治療開始後，她的身體曾迅速好轉，我們也重新燃起了希望。然而，命運似乎始終沒有停止考驗她。接連而來的疾病與打擊，一點一滴消耗著她的身體，也慢慢帶走了她曾經旺盛的生命力。"
  en="Although the tumor was successfully removed, the surgery left her permanently blind in her right eye. For a time we feared that she would lose the sight of her left eye as well, until cataracts were identified as the cause and restored through another operation. Hormone replacement therapy brought encouraging improvement, and hope gradually returned to our family. Yet life's trials were not over. One illness followed another, each quietly taking away a little more of the strength that had carried her through so many decades of sacrifice and perseverance."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="母親向來豁達，從不畏懼死亡。她一生經歷過太多艱難，早已學會坦然面對生命中的得失。然而，就在離世前兩個星期，我們像往常一樣視頻時，她卻忽然流下了眼淚。她說，她很想我，希望我早一點回家，不要等到過年，否則就見不到她了。那一刻，我仍以為她只是因病情反覆而感到悲觀，便安慰她說，再住幾天院，好好休養，很快就會好起來。沒有想到，那竟成了我們最後一次如此平靜地交談。"
  en="My mother had never been afraid of death. Life had taught her, over many decades, to accept both joy and sorrow with quiet courage. Yet two weeks before she passed away, during one of our regular video calls, something unexpected happened. She suddenly began to cry. She told me how much she missed me and asked me to come home sooner, not to wait until the Lunar New Year, because otherwise I would not see her again. I tried to reassure her, believing that her illness had simply left her discouraged. I told her that a few more days in the hospital and some rest would surely help. I never imagined that it would be our last peaceful conversation."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="直到今天，我仍常常想起那一次通話。如果當時我知道那真的是最後的告別，我一定會立刻放下手中的一切，飛回她的身邊。然而，人生最深的遺憾，往往並不是沒有努力，而是在事情發生的當下，我們並不知道，那一刻，就是最後一次。最後一次聽見她的聲音，最後一次看見她的笑容，最後一次對她說：「等我回家。」等到真正明白時，一切都已經成為回憶。"
  en="Even now, I often return to that conversation in my mind. Had I known it was our farewell, I would have laid aside everything and boarded the first flight home. But perhaps life's deepest regrets are seldom the result of unwillingness. More often, they come because we do not recognize the last time while we are living it. The last time we hear a familiar voice. The last time we see a beloved smile. The last time we say, 'I'll be home soon,' believing there will still be another tomorrow. Only afterward do we realize that an ordinary moment has quietly become a memory we will carry for the rest of our lives."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="母親年輕的時候，最喜歡聽老家一位基督徒長者講聖經故事。他也是我的本族爺爺，在那個信仰並不自由的年代，仍然默默把聖經中的人物、真理和盼望講給鄉親們聽。母親雖然沒有正式受洗，也沒有公開宣認自己的信仰，但我相信，那些故事早已像種子一樣埋藏在她心裡，潛移默化地塑造了她對生命、苦難與善良的理解。如今再回想母親的一生，我常常覺得，她身上那份寬厚、忍耐、憐憫與愛，早已流露出基督信仰所珍視的品格。"
  en="When my mother was young, she especially loved listening to Bible stories told by an elderly Christian from our hometown, a respected elder of our extended family. In an age when faith was seldom spoken of openly, he quietly shared the stories of Scripture and the hope they carried. Although my mother never formally became a Christian, I believe those stories took root in her heart like seeds planted in good soil. Over the years they quietly shaped the way she understood suffering, kindness, forgiveness, and hope. Looking back now, I cannot help seeing in her life many of the very virtues that the Christian faith holds most dear."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="至於我自己，則從青年時代漫長的求索，一步步走到了今日。孩提時曾接觸過福音，後來在人生的探索中，漸漸認識了基督，也接受了祂的救贖。我開始明白，死亡並不是生命的終點，而是另一個生命的開始。對相信基督的人而言，當地上的勞苦結束，靈魂便回到天父所預備的家中，等候基督再來，等候復活，也等候與所愛的人再次相聚。正因如此，我相信母親並沒有真正離開，只是比我先一步回到了天家。"
  en="My own journey followed a different path. From the questioning of youth to the quieter reflections of later life, I gradually came to know Christ and to embrace His redemption. Through Him I learned that death is not the end of life, but the doorway to a greater one. For those who belong to Christ, earthly labor is not the final chapter. Beyond it lies the Father's house, where His people await the resurrection, the return of Christ, and the joyful reunion of all who are His. It is this hope that allows me to believe my mother has not been lost to me forever. She has simply gone home before I did."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="有人曾問我：「你怎麼能確定神存在？又怎麼知道自己所相信的是獨一的真神？」坦白說，我沒有辦法用數學公式或科學實驗去證明神，也不認為信仰最終建立在這樣的證明之上。信仰固然可以思考，也可以討論，但它最終仍是一種回應——當人願意向真理敞開自己的心，願意承認自己的有限，願意尋求那位創造生命的主時，信心便在人的生命裡慢慢生長。"
  en="People sometimes ask me, 'How can you be certain that God exists? And how do you know that the God you believe in is the true God?' My honest answer is that I cannot prove God's existence by mathematical formulas or scientific experiments, nor do I believe that genuine faith ultimately rests upon such proof. Faith welcomes honest questions and thoughtful reflection, but in the end it is a response of the heart. It begins when we are willing to acknowledge our own limitations, to seek the truth with humility, and to open ourselves to the One who gave us life."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="如果神真的存在，那麼相信祂的人所得著的，是永恆的生命、永遠的盼望，以及死亡不能奪去的喜樂；如果神不存在，那麼因著信仰，我依然學會了盼望、自律、饒恕、愛人如己，也努力作鹽作光，讓別人的生命因我而多一分溫暖。無論如何，真正的信仰都不是靠爭辯得勝，而是在每天的生活裡活出來。生命本身，才是信仰最有力的見證。"
  en="If God truly exists, then those who trust in Him receive a hope that reaches beyond death itself—eternal life, abiding joy, and a promise that nothing in this world can take away. If, on the other hand, I were mistaken, I would still be grateful for a faith that has taught me hope instead of despair, self-discipline instead of self-indulgence, forgiveness instead of resentment, and love instead of indifference. Such a life would hardly be wasted. In the end, faith is not validated because it wins every argument, but because it transforms the one who lives it. A life shaped by grace is the most persuasive testimony faith can offer."
%}
