</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html

  en="A stable nation depends upon a harmonious society, and a harmonious society is built upon healthy families. How, then, can a family preserve harmony? How should parents guide the next generation? <h2 class="essay-section">
一、孝心無價
</h2>
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="兒女都要知道，贍養、孝敬父母是國家法律和兒女的義務，是中華民族的傳統美德。生兒育女，父母恩重如山。人的一生，從小長大，到成家立業，父母對兒女撫養、教育付出的心血無法估量，歷盡千辛萬苦，母親甚至要闖鬼門關。想到這些，兒女報父母恩怎麼做也不過分。兒女要真情實意地善待老人，這是天意，萬萬不可違背。然而，大千世界無奇不有。忘恩負義、不孝之子並不少見，甚至打爹罵娘的也有。這與人不講道理、不知感恩是分不開的。要知道，兒女今天善待父母，就是善待自己的未來。"
  en="Every son and daughter should remember that honoring and caring for one's parents is not only a moral obligation, but also a duty recognized by society and upheld by law. In Chinese culture, filial devotion has long been regarded as the foundation of family life. From infancy to adulthood, from the first day of school to the day a child establishes a home of his or her own, parents devote immeasurable love, sacrifice, and labor to the lives of their children. A mother even risks her own life in bringing new life into the world. Such kindness can never truly be repaid. Children should therefore care for their parents with sincere gratitude rather than mere obligation. Sadly, not everyone does. In every generation there are those who forget the sacrifices that made their own lives possible. Some even turn against the very parents who raised them. Such behavior grows from a heart that has forgotten gratitude. In the end, the way we treat our parents today becomes the example by which our own children may one day treat us."

What matters most is knowing that they are equally cherished. While most parents naturally strive to treat their children fairly, those who knowingly favor one child over another risk sowing resentment that may linger long after childhood has passed. <h2 class="essay-section">
一、愛而不溺，嚴而不苛

<h2 class="essay-section">
九、善與人處，和與善人處
</h2>
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="人的一生，無論在什麼地方學習或工作，總要和人打交道。要幹好一項事業，因素是多方面的，其中一個重要方面就是處理好人際關係，營造一個和諧環境。在這方面孩子今後需要應酬的事很多，但是，要特別注意以下幾點。"
  en="No one lives or works in isolation. Wherever life may lead, success depends not only upon knowledge and ability but also upon the ability to live and work well with others. Healthy relationships create an environment in which people can cooperate, grow, and accomplish far more than they could alone. For this reason, parents should also teach their children the principles of good character and healthy human relationships. The following reflections concern several qualities that deserve particular attention throughout life."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="1．與善人處。 「近朱者赤，近墨者黑」，自己在任何單位學習或工作，一定要注意團結大多數人。在這樣的基礎上，自己和部分人接近得多一些，成為你的知音，這是人之常情的事。但是，他們必須是德才兼備的人。簡要地說，他們得刻苦學習，扎實工作，品德高尚，遵紀守法，寬厚待人，誠信可靠。在接觸過程中，大家都能做到相互學習、取長補短，不斷完善自我。彼此互相關心，互相幫助，增進友誼，和諧相處，團結奮進，共同提高。"
  en="1. Choose Your Companions Wisely. An old Chinese saying observes that people are shaped by the company they keep. Wherever we study or work, we should strive to live in harmony with everyone while also seeking the friendship of those whose character inspires us. True friends are not chosen because they are successful or influential, but because they are honest, diligent, trustworthy, and kind. They challenge us to become better people through their own example. Friendships built upon integrity encourage mutual learning, sincere support, and shared growth. Such relationships enrich not only our own lives but also strengthen every community to which we belong."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="2．詩化其心。 在任何一個單位中，人的生活條件，學習、工作態度和表現會有很大差異。父母教育孩子無論到什麼單位，在學習和工作上都要向先進學習，在生活享受上要和自己條件差的人相比。人生不如意的事十有八九，因此，要有樂觀的心態，詩化其心，方覺人世之美好，才能調整好自己的心態，廣交有志之士，激勵自己奮發有為。"
  en="2. Keep the Heart Poetic. Life is never perfectly equal. Wherever we go, we will meet people whose circumstances are better than our own and others who face greater hardships. Wisdom lies in learning from those who excel while remaining grateful for what we ourselves have received. A hopeful spirit allows us to discover beauty even amid life's imperfections. To 'keep the heart poetic' is to preserve gratitude, optimism, and the ability to see goodness despite disappointment. Such an outlook not only brings inner peace but also attracts companions who encourage us to pursue lives of purpose and generosity."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="3．善解人意，才能善於待人。父母教育孩子在人際交往中，要善解人意，善於待人，多為別人做好事，多成人之美，這是每個人應具備的美德。但是，自己無論為誰做好事，絕不違紀，更不違法。"
  en="3. Understanding Others Is the Beginning of Kindness. One cannot truly treat others well without first learning to understand them. Parents should encourage their children to be thoughtful, considerate, and willing to help wherever help is needed. A generous heart delights not only in doing good, but also in rejoicing when others succeed. Such kindness is among the finest qualities a person can cultivate. Yet compassion should always be guided by wisdom and integrity. No act of kindness should require compromising one's principles or violating the law. True goodness is both warmhearted and upright."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="4．交情交情，要交真情。孩子無論和親友相處，還是和外人交往，物質上的禮尚往來是必要的，是人之常情的事。然而，要把交情放到首位，錢和財沒有不行，但是，不能過分計較它，更不能為錢和財影響人際關係。無功不受祿，警鐘要長鳴，不義之財要不得，要防微杜漸，不可妄為。"
  en="4. Let Friendship Be Founded on Sincerity. Whether dealing with relatives, close friends, or strangers, courtesy and generosity have their proper place. Gifts may express goodwill, but they should never become the foundation of a relationship. Genuine friendship rests upon trust, sincerity, and mutual respect rather than material exchange. Wealth has its value, yet it should never outweigh integrity or damage human relationships. Likewise, one should never accept undeserved benefits or seek dishonest gain. A clear conscience is worth far more than any material advantage, and small compromises in character should never be allowed to grow into greater ones."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="5．面對糾紛，能屈能伸。由於各種原因，在人際關係中有分歧意見，甚至產生矛盾是難以避免的。在處理上述問題時，要把下列幾點意見貫穿解決問題的全過程。"
  en="5. Face Conflict with Wisdom and Grace. Differences of opinion and personal conflicts are inevitable wherever people live and work together. The question is not whether disagreements will arise, but how we choose to respond when they do. In resolving conflict, patience, wisdom, and self-restraint are often more powerful than anger or pride. The following principles, in my experience, are worth remembering whenever misunderstandings or disputes occur."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="有理要讓人，無理要服人，要寬宏大量，能容人。「人貴有自知之明」，每個人都應該想想自己的弱點、缺點或錯誤，先作自我批評而後再要求別人。彼此多一份自責，多一份理解和諒解，人際關係就多一份寬鬆，多一份和諧。"
  en="When we are right, we should remain gracious; when we are wrong, we should have the humility to admit it. True strength is measured not by winning every argument, but by the generosity to forgive and the courage to examine oneself honestly. Self-awareness is among the greatest of human virtues. Before judging others, we should first acknowledge our own weaknesses and mistakes. The more willing we are to practice self-reflection, understanding, and forgiveness, the more harmonious our relationships will become."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html

Children should learn to remain composed, think carefully, and exercise patience before reacting in anger. Respecting others should never require surrendering one's own dignity or legitimate rights. True wisdom knows both when to yield and when to stand firm."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="冤家宜解，不宜結；除冤方能避禍。人只要堅持這樣的理念處理糾紛，情緒就能穩定，坦誠對話，加強溝通，矛盾化解，問題和平解決，增強信任，和睦相處，人際關係良性發展，人和方能萬事興。"
  en="Whenever possible, conflicts should be resolved rather than allowed to harden into lasting resentment. Reconciliation is almost always wiser than revenge. Honest conversation, mutual respect, and sincere communication build trust where suspicion once existed. When people seek understanding instead of victory, disagreements gradually give way to cooperation, relationships are restored, and communities become stronger. Lasting prosperity is built not merely upon talent or wealth, but upon people who know how to live together in peace."

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="綜上所述，待人寬容、禮讓、忍讓是一個人道德修養一方面的體現，而不是軟弱可欺，更不是害怕邪惡。寬容、禮讓、忍讓也是為了更好地開展鬥爭，為了把握好策略，爭取鬥爭的主動權。開展鬥爭，自己要遵紀守法，做到有理、有利、有節，始終保持清醒頭腦，不可蠻幹。自己率先講文明，擺事實，講道理，揭露問題、批判錯誤，扶正壓邪，爭取大多數人的同情和支持。這樣，自己就能團結大多數，依靠大多數，促進人際關係良性發展，營造友好和諧環境，以有利於自己的學習和工作。"
  en="In the end, tolerance, courtesy, and patience should never be mistaken for weakness. They are signs of maturity, self-discipline, and moral strength. Problems are best resolved through reason rather than impulse, through evidence rather than accusation, and through justice rather than resentment. Those who act with fairness, dignity, and compassion are far more likely to earn the trust and support of others. In doing so, they help create communities where cooperation flourishes, relationships endure, and every individual has a better opportunity to learn, work, and live in peace."
