<h1 class="essay-title">父親</h1>

<p class="essay-subtitle">
<em>My Father</em>
</p>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="父親自幼篤志求學，然而家境清寒，又生長於偏遠農村，求學之路遠比常人艱難。高中時，他就讀聊城三中，每逢返校，都要在夜裡徒步九個多小時。一路穿過鄉村田野，迎著寒風，也迎著黎明。他經歷了戰後貧困和三年困難時期，卻始終沒有放棄讀書的志向，最終成為我們家族第一位走出農村、考入大學的大學生。"
  en="From an early age, my father believed that learning could change the course of a person's life. Born into poverty in a remote village, he pursued an education under circumstances that would have discouraged many others. While attending Liaocheng No. 3 High School, he often walked through the night for more than nine hours to return to campus. Along lonely country roads, through winter winds and before the first light of dawn, he carried little more than books, determination, and hope. Having endured the hardships of postwar poverty and the Great Chinese Famine, he eventually became the first person in our family to leave the countryside and earn a university education."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="求學期間，父親一直是班幹部，畢業前夕加入中國共產黨。步入教育工作後，他先後擔任教導主任、副校長和黨委書記，服務於四所高中。無論走到哪裡，他總能帶領師生奮發圖強，使原本沉寂的校園重新煥發生機。多年之後，桃李遍佈縣城，許多人提起他的名字，仍懷著由衷的敬意。教育，並不是他的一份職業，而是他畢生的使命。"
  en="Leadership came naturally to him. He served as a student leader throughout high school and university, and shortly before graduation he joined the Chinese Communist Party, a path that reflected both the ideals and the realities of his generation. During the decades that followed, he devoted his life to education, serving successively as Director of Academic Affairs, Vice Principal, and Party Secretary at four different high schools. Wherever he went, struggling schools found new purpose, teachers discovered renewed confidence, and generations of students were inspired to pursue lives beyond the limits of the small county where they had grown up. For my father, education was never merely a profession. It was the calling to which he devoted his entire life."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="父親自幼深受孔孟文化薰陶，相信一個人應當立志、自律、承擔責任；而在家庭裡，父親的權威是不容置疑的。對外，他肩負著學校與家庭的重擔；對內，他把全部希望都寄託在我的身上。也正因如此，他對我的要求近乎苛刻。多年以後我才明白，那些嚴厲並非出於冷漠，而是出於一位父親對兒子最深的期待，只是當時的我，還沒有能力理解。"
  en="My father was deeply shaped by the teachings of Confucius and Mencius. He believed that a person's life should be marked by discipline, responsibility, and unwavering commitment, and that within a family a father's authority carried both privilege and obligation. He bore the burdens of his school and of our household with equal seriousness, and he placed his greatest hopes upon me. His expectations were demanding—at times they seemed almost impossible to satisfy. Only many years later did I realize that beneath his sternness was not a lack of love, but a father's profound hope that his son might become stronger than he himself had ever been."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="在外人眼中，我一直是父親引以為傲的兒子；而在家裡，我卻很早便戴上了求學的『緊箍咒』。他的教誨日復一日，幾乎從未停止。少年時，我一次又一次地反抗，也一次又一次地懷疑自己的價值。我曾以為，他從未真正理解過我；直到後來離開家鄉、走進社會，在責任與壓力面前跌跌撞撞，我才逐漸發現，父親並不是要替我安排人生，而是在用他所知道的方式，預備我去面對一個遠比家庭更加艱難的世界。"
  en="Outside our home, my father spoke of me with unmistakable pride. Inside, however, I often felt bound by the relentless expectations that surrounded my education. His counsel, his reminders, and his corrections seemed endless. As a young man I resisted them, sometimes openly, sometimes only in my heart, and there were moments when I wondered whether he truly understood me at all. It was only after I left home, entered the wider world, and began carrying responsibilities of my own that I slowly recognized what he had been trying to do. He was not attempting to live my life for me. He was preparing me, in the only way he knew, for a world far more demanding than the shelter of home."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="孔子說：「道不行，乘桴浮於海。」年輕時，我對自由有著近乎本能的渴望。那份渴望，如同一種來自遠方的呼召，把我帶到了太平洋彼岸。在陌生的國度裡，我一點一滴建立自己的事業，也建立自己的家庭，努力讓自己成為一棵能夠抵擋風雨的大樹。然而，就在我漸漸站穩腳步的時候，父親卻已年華老去，再也無法像從前那樣，看著我一步一步成長。"
  en="Confucius once wrote, 'When the Way does not prevail, I shall set out upon a raft and drift upon the sea.' In my youth, I was driven by an almost irresistible longing for freedom. Looking back now, that longing feels less like youthful rebellion than a calling that carried me across the Pacific Ocean. In a distant land I slowly built a career, raised a family, and learned how to stand on my own. By the time I had finally grown into the person I hoped to become, my father had already entered the twilight of his life. He would never again have the chance to watch me grow, as he once had from only a few steps away."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="隨著年歲漸長，我也開始明白，自由並不是毫無約束，而是願意承擔約束；安全感也不是來自世界，而是來自自己所願意承擔的責任。我曾經以為父親為我築起的是牢籠，後來才知道，那其實是一道替我抵擋風雨的圍牆。就在這樣的歲月裡，我終於與父親完全和解。每次回國，他總是發自內心地高興。雖然仍會勸我回國發展，卻從來沒有要求我按照他的意思生活。他尊重了我的選擇，而我，也終於讀懂了他的愛。"
  en="As the years passed, I also came to understand that freedom is not the absence of restraint, but the willingness to embrace responsibility. Security is seldom something the world gives us; more often it is something we build through commitment and sacrifice. What I once believed to be a cage my father had built around me, I now recognize as a wall that sheltered me from storms I could not yet foresee. Somewhere along that journey, without either of us speaking of it directly, we became fully reconciled. Whenever I returned to China, his joy was unmistakable. Though he sometimes reminded me that I could have built a good life in my homeland, he never tried to make my decisions for me. He honored the path I had chosen, and at last I understood the love that had guided him all along."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="晚年的父親一直患有高血壓、冠心病和持續性心房顫動。今年四月感染新冠後，身體便一直沒有真正恢復，呼吸功能逐漸衰退，大部分時間只能臥床休息。八月再次感染後，心力衰竭接踵而來，呼吸與消化功能也日益惡化。曾經步履堅定、意志堅強的人，終究還是慢慢被歲月奪去了力量。"
  en="In his later years, my father lived with hypertension, coronary artery disease, and persistent atrial fibrillation. After contracting COVID-19 in April, he never fully regained his strength. His breathing became increasingly labored, and he spent much of his remaining time confined to bed. A second infection in August led to heart failure, followed by a steady decline in both his respiratory and digestive functions. The man whose determination had carried him through decades of hardship gradually surrendered his strength to the quiet advance of age and illness."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="然而，疾病並沒有奪去他的從容。直到生命最後的時刻，他依然神志清醒，平靜地安排自己的身後之事，沒有恐懼，也沒有慌亂。他接受了生命終將走到盡頭的事實，就像一位完成漫長旅程的人，整理好行囊，安然踏上最後一段路。那一刻，我看到的不是一位被疾病擊倒的老人，而是一位完成了自己使命的人。"
  en="Yet illness never robbed him of his composure. Until the very end, his mind remained clear. Calmly and deliberately, he made the necessary arrangements for those he would leave behind. There was neither fear nor confusion in him—only a quiet acceptance that his journey on earth was drawing to its close. It reminded me of a traveler who, after a long road faithfully walked, packs his belongings one final time before setting out for home. In those last days, I did not see a man defeated by disease. I saw a man who had finished the work entrusted to him."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="如今，父親的房間已經空了。陪伴他多年的衣物和隨身用品，都隨著他的離去一一收起。每次走到那扇門前，我總會不由自主地停下腳步。房間裡沒有聲音，沒有身影，卻彷彿仍然保留著他的氣息。那些共同走過的歲月，那些曾經的嚴厲與慈愛、爭執與理解、期待與和解，都在沉默中一一浮現。死亡帶走了一個人的身體，卻帶不走他留在家人生命裡的一切。"
  en="Now my father's room stands quietly empty. The clothes he once wore and the everyday belongings that had accompanied him for so many years have all been carefully put away. Yet whenever I pass his door, I still find myself slowing down. The room is silent, but it does not feel empty. It seems to hold an invisible presence, as though the life once lived there has not entirely departed. Memories return without being summoned—years of discipline and affection, conflict and understanding, expectation and reconciliation. Death may remove a person from our sight, but it cannot erase the life he has written into the hearts of those who remain."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="如今再回首，父親留給我的，早已不只是童年的記憶，更是一種看待人生的方式。他教會我勤奮、責任、堅持，也教會我在逆境中不輕言放棄。年輕時，我總想掙脫他的影子；多年以後才發現，他早已成為我生命的一部分。時間彷彿停留在他八十八歲生日的那個秋天。秋風吹過庭院，一切都歸於寧靜，而那些屬於父親的故事，也從歲月走進了永恆。"
  en="Looking back now, what my father left me is far more than the memories of childhood. He left me a way of facing life itself—one shaped by diligence, responsibility, perseverance, and the quiet resolve never to surrender in the face of hardship. As a young man I spent much of my energy trying to step out of his shadow. Only much later did I realize that his character had already become part of my own. In my memory, time seems forever suspended in the autumn of his eighty-eighth birthday. The autumn wind still moves gently through the courtyard, and everything has fallen into silence. Yet the story of his life has not ended. It has simply passed from the years of this world into eternity."
%}
