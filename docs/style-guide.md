# Xinghe Edition Style Guide

## Design Philosophy

The design should retreat, allowing the words to speak.

版面退後，讓文字說話。

---

## Principles

- Chinese is the primary text; English is a faithful literary translation.
- Paragraph-by-paragraph bilingual presentation.
- Chinese on the left, English on the right.
- Mobile devices stack vertically.
- Generous whitespace.
- Thin light-gray separators.
- Serif typography.
- No decorative cards.
- No unnecessary colors.
- No visual effects that distract from reading.
