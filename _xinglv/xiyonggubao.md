<h1 class="essay-title">
湖畔的石頭史詩──西庸古堡
</h1>

<p class="essay-subtitle">
<em>A Stone Epic on the Lakeshore — Chillon Castle</em>
</p>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="西庸古堡（Château de Chillon）位於瑞士西南部日內瓦湖（Lake Geneva）東岸，坐落於蒙特勒（Montreux）附近的一塊天然岩石小島上。城堡三面環水，一面與陸地相連，自十二世紀以來一直扼守著通往阿爾卑斯山的重要通道，是連接義大利與西歐商旅、朝聖者和軍隊的交通咽喉，因此具有極高的戰略價值。"
  en="On the eastern shore of Lake Geneva, where the deep blue water meets the foothills of the Alps, Chillon Castle rises from a natural rocky islet like a fortress from another age. Surrounded by water on three sides and linked to the mainland by a narrow causeway, it has guarded one of the principal routes through the Alps since the twelfth century. Merchants, pilgrims, and armies traveling between Italy and Western Europe all passed beneath its watch, making Chillon one of the most strategically important strongholds in medieval Switzerland."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="西庸古堡最早可追溯至十一世紀，後來成為薩伏依伯爵（House of Savoy）的重要堡壘與官邸。薩伏依家族控制著阿爾卑斯山的重要山口，憑藉商路徵稅而日益強盛。今日遊客所見的大部分建築，形成於十三世紀，是典型的中世紀防禦城堡。"
  en="The origins of Chillon date back to the eleventh century. It later became a principal fortress and residence of the House of Savoy, whose rulers grew wealthy and influential by controlling the Alpine passes and collecting tolls from the flourishing trade routes. Most of the buildings seen today were completed during the thirteenth century, preserving one of Europe's finest examples of a medieval defensive castle."
%}

<h2 class="essay-section">走進中世紀古堡</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="走進古堡，首先映入眼簾的是一座座石砌塔樓。塔樓既是瞭望台，也是最後的防線，可以俯瞰整個日內瓦湖及湖岸道路。站在塔樓上，遠處是終年積雪的阿爾卑斯群峰，近處是碧藍的湖水，很容易理解當年的守軍如何及早發現來自湖面或山谷的敵人。"
  en="Stepping through the castle gates, visitors are immediately surrounded by massive stone towers that once served both as watchtowers and as the fortress's final line of defense. From their heights, sweeping views extend across Lake Geneva to the snow-covered Alps beyond. Looking over the tranquil water and the narrow lakeside road below, it is easy to imagine medieval sentries spotting approaching travelers—or invading armies—long before they reached the castle walls."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="古堡內陳列著大量中世紀武器，包括長劍、戰斧、長矛、十字弓、盾牌以及各式盔甲。這些兵器見證了冷兵器時代的戰爭，也反映出當時騎士文化與封建制度。厚重的鎧甲雖然提供良好的保護，卻限制了士兵的靈活性；而十字弓則大大改變了戰場格局，使受過長期訓練的騎士不再具有絕對優勢。"
  en="The castle also houses an impressive collection of medieval weapons, including longswords, battle axes, spears, crossbows, shields, and suits of armor. Together they tell the story of an age when warfare depended on steel, skill, and personal courage. Heavy armor offered remarkable protection at the cost of mobility, while the crossbow revolutionized the battlefield by enabling ordinary soldiers to challenge heavily trained knights from a distance."
%}
<h2 class="essay-section">地牢與自由</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="城堡最令人印象深刻的，是位於地下的巨大地牢。粗大的石柱直接支撐著整座城堡，昏暗潮濕的空間令人感受到中世紀監獄的壓抑。這裡曾關押過政治犯、宗教犯以及戰俘，其中最著名的是日內瓦修道院院長弗朗索瓦·博尼瓦（François Bonivard）。"
  en="The most unforgettable part of Chillon Castle lies beneath the fortress itself. Descending into the vast underground dungeon, visitors find themselves among towering stone columns that have supported the castle for centuries. The cool, dimly lit chambers still evoke the weight and silence of a medieval prison. Over the years, political prisoners, religious dissidents, and prisoners of war were confined here. Among them, none is remembered more vividly than François Bonivard, the Prior of Geneva."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="博尼瓦因支持日內瓦擺脫薩伏依公國的統治，於 1530 年被囚禁於西庸古堡長達四年。他被鐵鏈鎖在石柱上，只能圍繞著柱子行走，地面至今仍可看到長年摩擦留下的痕跡。1536 年，伯恩軍隊攻佔古堡，博尼瓦終於獲得自由。"
  en="Because he supported Geneva's struggle for independence from the House of Savoy, Bonivard was imprisoned at Chillon in 1530 and remained there for four long years. Chained to one of the massive stone pillars, he could do little more than walk in circles around it. Even today, visitors can still see the worn groove in the stone floor, quietly preserved by the passage of countless lonely footsteps. In 1536, Bernese forces captured the castle, and Bonivard finally regained his freedom."
%}

<h2 class="essay-section">拜倫與《西庸的囚徒》</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="這段經歷後來深深打動了英國浪漫主義詩人喬治·戈登·拜倫（Lord Byron）。1816 年，拜倫遊覽西庸古堡後，以博尼瓦的故事創作了長詩《西庸的囚徒》（The Prisoner of Chillon），使這座原本屬於地方歷史的城堡聞名於世界。"
  en="Nearly three centuries later, Bonivard's story profoundly moved the English Romantic poet Lord Byron. During his visit to Chillon Castle in 1816, Byron found inspiration in the prisoner's fate and wrote his celebrated narrative poem <em>The Prisoner of Chillon</em>. Through his poetry, what had once been a local chapter of Swiss history became part of the literary imagination of the world."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="拜倫當年還親自在囚禁博尼瓦的那根石柱上刻下自己的名字「Byron」。如今，這個簽名依然保留在柱子上，成為古堡最著名的文學遺跡之一。可惜我離開古堡後才知道這件事，因此沒有特地回去尋找這個刻痕，也算是此次旅程留下的一個小小遺憾。"
  en="Before leaving the castle, Byron carved his own name—<em>“Byron”</em>—into the very pillar to which Bonivard had once been chained. The inscription remains there today, one of Chillon's most treasured literary relics. Unfortunately, I did not learn of its existence until after I had already left the castle. By then it was too late to go back, leaving behind one small but memorable regret from an otherwise unforgettable journey."
%}
{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="今天的西庸古堡早已失去軍事功能，但它仍靜靜矗立在日內瓦湖畔。湖光山色賦予它童話般的美麗，而厚重的石牆、冰冷的地牢與拜倫的詩篇，又使它承載著自由、信念與人性的記憶。這座城堡不僅是一件中世紀建築傑作，更是一部用石頭寫成的歐洲歷史。"
  en="Today, Chillon Castle no longer guards an Alpine passage, yet it has lost none of its presence. Reflected in the tranquil waters of Lake Geneva, it possesses the timeless beauty of a fairy tale. Behind its massive stone walls, however, linger stories of imprisonment, conviction, and the enduring human longing for freedom. Byron's poem transformed those memories into literature, while the castle itself continues to preserve them in stone. More than a masterpiece of medieval architecture, Chillon Castle is a chronicle of Europe carved in stone."
%}
