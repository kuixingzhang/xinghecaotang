{% include xinghe/paragraph.html

<h2 class="essay-section">什麼是齒軌火車？</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="普通火車依靠車輪與鋼軌之間的摩擦力前進，但坡度過陡時，摩擦力不足，車輪容易打滑。"
  en="Unlike conventional railways, which rely solely on the friction between steel wheels and rails, mountain railways face a unique challenge. On steep slopes, friction alone is no longer sufficient, and the wheels can easily lose their grip."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="因此，瑞士工程師在兩條鋼軌中央增設一條齒軌（Rack Rail），列車底部裝有齒輪（Pinion），與齒軌互相咬合。即使坡度高達 25%，列車仍能安全、平穩地攀登，因此少女峰鐵路也成為世界最著名的齒軌鐵路之一。"
  en="Swiss engineers solved this problem with an ingenious rack-and-pinion system. A toothed rail is installed between the two running rails, while a gear beneath the train meshes securely with the rack. This design enables the railway to climb gradients of up to 25 percent with remarkable stability, making the Jungfrau Railway one of the world's best-known examples of mountain rail engineering."
%}

<h2 class="essay-section">山上的主要景點</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="抵達少女峰山坳後，可以參觀："
  en="Upon arriving at Jungfraujoch, visitors will discover that the destination offers far more than spectacular mountain views. Several attractions await within and around Europe's highest railway station:"
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="• **斯芬克斯觀景台（Sphinx Observatory）**：俯瞰阿萊奇冰川（Aletsch Glacier）。<br>
• **冰宮（Ice Palace）**：建於冰川內部，可欣賞各式冰雕。<br>
• **冰雪平台（Plateau）**：全年可近距離接觸積雪與冰川。<br>
• **Lindt 巧克力旗艦店**：號稱世界上海拔最高的 Lindt 商店。"
  en="• <strong>Sphinx Observatory</strong> — An observation platform with sweeping views of the Aletsch Glacier, the largest glacier in the Alps.<br>
• <strong>Ice Palace</strong> — A network of tunnels carved into the glacier itself, where intricate ice sculptures shimmer beneath the frozen surface.<br>
• <strong>Plateau</strong> — An open snowfield that allows visitors to experience year-round snow and ice at close range.<br>
• <strong>Lindt Flagship Store</strong> — Widely promoted as the highest-altitude Lindt chocolate shop in the world."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="正因為集壯麗山岳、百年工程奇蹟、現代纜車與世界最高鐵路車站於一身，少女峰之旅不僅是一趟登山觀光，更是一場結合自然景觀與工程智慧的經典阿爾卑斯體驗。"
  en="Bringing together majestic Alpine landscapes, a century-old engineering masterpiece, state-of-the-art cable-car technology, and Europe's highest railway station, a journey to Jungfraujoch is far more than a sightseeing excursion. It is an unforgettable Alpine experience where the grandeur of nature and the ingenuity of human engineering come together in remarkable harmony."
%}
<h2 class="essay-section">「Top of Europe」是什麼？</h2>

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="許多人以為火車已經抵達少女峰山頂，其實並非如此。"
  en="Many first-time visitors assume that the railway reaches the summit of Jungfrau. In fact, it does not."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="列車真正抵達的是少女峰山坳（Jungfraujoch），位於少女峰（Jungfrau）與僧侶峰（Mönch）之間的山口，海拔 3,454 公尺。這裡建有歐洲最高的鐵路車站，因此被稱為 **Top of Europe（歐洲之巔）**。這是一個旅遊品牌名稱，並不是表示到達了歐洲最高峰。"
  en="The railway actually terminates at Jungfraujoch, the mountain saddle between Jungfrau and the Mönch, at an elevation of 3,454 meters (11,332 feet). Home to Europe's highest railway station, it has long been promoted as the <strong>Top of Europe</strong>. The phrase is a tourism brand rather than a geographical description—it celebrates the remarkable location of the station, not the highest summit on the continent."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="真正的歐洲最高峰一般認為是位於高加索山脈、俄羅斯境內的厄爾布魯士峰（Mount Elbrus），海拔 5,642 公尺；若僅計算阿爾卑斯山，最高峰則是法國與義大利交界的白朗峰（Mont Blanc），海拔約 4,806 公尺。"
  en="Europe's highest mountain is generally recognized as Mount Elbrus in the Caucasus Mountains of Russia, rising to 5,642 meters (18,510 feet). Within the Alps themselves, however, the highest peak is Mont Blanc, which stands on the border between France and Italy at approximately 4,806 meters (15,768 feet)."
%}
