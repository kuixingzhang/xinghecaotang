<h1 class="essay-title">蘭切里亞瀑布</h1>

<p>
蘭切里亞瀑布步道並不長，全程約兩英里，步道平緩，走起來原本應該十分輕鬆。然而我剛開始走在平地上，竟已有些氣喘，或許是剛從接近海平面的<strong>弗雷斯諾</strong>，一路驅車來到海拔約七千二百英尺（2195米）的<strong>內華達山脈</strong>，身體還來不及適應。
</p>

<p>
<em>
The Rancheria Falls Trail is not a long hike. Stretching only about two miles round trip, it follows a gentle grade and should be an easy walk. Yet I found myself short of breath almost from the very beginning. Having driven from near sea level in <strong>Fresno</strong> to an elevation of about 7,200 feet (2,195 m) in the <strong>Sierra Nevada</strong>, my body had not yet adjusted to the thinner mountain air.
</em>
</p>

<p>
到了步道盡頭的瀑布腳下，其實真正的探索才剛剛開始。若想登上瀑布頂端，便只能循著遊人踏出的原始山路向上攀爬。沒有步道，沒有木階，也沒有護欄，六十餘度的岩坡只能手腳並用，借著裸露的花崗岩和盤根錯節的樹根一步步向上。山頂依舊保持著原始風貌，林木繁茂，沒有人工修整的痕跡，彷彿仍停留在幾百萬年前的模樣。
</p>

<p>
<em>
At the foot of the waterfall, where the official trail ends, the real adventure begins. Anyone wishing to reach the top must follow a rough path worn by previous hikers. There are no constructed trails, no wooden steps, and no handrails. The granite slope, rising at more than sixty degrees, demands the use of both hands and feet, climbing carefully over exposed rock and tangled tree roots. Above the falls, the forest remains remarkably untouched, dense with towering trees and almost entirely free of human alteration, as though the landscape had been preserved from millions of years ago.
</em>
</p>

![蘭切里亞瀑布](/assets/images/xinglv/rancheria1.jpg)

<p>
蘭切里亞瀑布坐落於內華達山脈西坡，瀑布所匯聚的溪水，主要來自冬季積雪與春夏融雪。每年五月、六月，積雪大量消融，溪流奔騰而下，形成高約一百五十英尺（約四十六米）的多級飛瀑；到了八月，雪水漸盡，溪流回歸平緩，因此瀑布也少了春日奔騰咆哮的氣勢。
</p>

<p>
<em>
Rancheria Falls lies on the western slope of the Sierra Nevada. Its waters are fed primarily by winter snowpack and the melting snows of spring and early summer. During May and June, snowmelt surges down the mountainside, creating a series of cascades with a total drop of about 150 feet (46 m). By August, however, the snowmelt has largely subsided, and the waterfall loses much of its springtime fury, returning to the quieter rhythm of a mountain stream.
</em>
</p>

<p>
攀上瀑布頂端，白雲悠悠，鳥鳴喈喈，溪水在花崗岩上蜿蜒流淌，叮咚作響，忽然躍下岩壁，白浪懸空。然而下來後站在瀑布下方的巨石之上，水霧迎面撲來，清爽無比；迎著陽光，道道彩虹映入眼簾。溪流繼續沿著花崗岩流瀉而下，遠處則是一望無際的松杉覆蓋群山，層巒疊翠，清幽開闊，令人流連忘返。
</p>

<p>
<em>
From the top of the falls, white clouds drift lazily overhead while birds fill the forest with song. The creek winds gently across smooth granite, murmuring softly before plunging suddenly over the cliff in a curtain of white water. Returning to the base, I stood upon a massive boulder as cool mist swept across my face. In the morning sunlight, delicate rainbows shimmered in the spray. Beyond the cascading water, the stream continued its descent over polished granite, while endless ridges of pine-covered mountains stretched into the distance. The serenity and grandeur of the landscape made it difficult to leave.
</em>
</p>

<p>
這裡並非著名景點，遊人卻遠比我想像中多，三五成群，不時有人坐在岩石上戲水、拍照。短短一個多小時，我至少遇見了五六十位遊客，頗有些出乎意料。
</p>

<p>
<em>
Although Rancheria Falls is far from being a famous destination, it attracted far more visitors than I had expected. Small groups gathered on the granite slabs, some photographing the falls while others cooled their feet in the clear mountain water. In little more than an hour, I encountered at least fifty or sixty hikers—an unexpectedly lively scene in what I had imagined would be a secluded corner of the Sierra.
</em>
</p>
