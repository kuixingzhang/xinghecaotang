{% include xinghe/paragraph.html

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="優勝美地瀑布（Yosemite Falls）位於美國加州優勝美地國家公園，是北美最高、也是世界最著名的瀑布之一。全瀑由上瀑、中段急流及下瀑三部分組成，總落差約七百四十米。每年冬末春初，內華達山脈積雪開始消融，無數雪水匯入優勝美地溪，繼而從花崗岩峭壁傾瀉而下，形成震耳欲聾的萬丈飛瀑。瀑布飛流直下，如白龍出谷，水霧瀰漫，陽光照射之下，時常可見彩虹橫跨山谷，蔚為奇觀。"
  en="Located in Yosemite National Park in California, Yosemite Falls is the tallest waterfall in North America and one of the world's most celebrated natural wonders. The falls consist of three sections—the Upper Fall, the Middle Cascades, and the Lower Fall—with a combined vertical drop of approximately 740 meters. Each year, as winter gives way to spring, melting snow from the Sierra Nevada pours into Yosemite Creek before plunging over immense granite cliffs in a thunderous cascade. The waterfall descends like a white dragon bursting from the mountains, filling the air with mist where brilliant rainbows often appear beneath the afternoon sun."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="優勝美地瀑布不僅是優勝美地山谷最醒目的地標，也是美國自然保護史上的重要象徵。早在歐洲人到來之前，世代居住於山谷中的阿瓦尼奇人（Ahwahneechee）便將這片山川視為神聖之地，瀑布奔流的轟鳴被認為蘊含著自然與生命的力量。1851年，首批進入優勝美地山谷的歐洲探險者首次目睹這座飛瀑，無不為其磅礴氣勢所震撼。此後，攝影先驅卡爾頓·沃特金斯（Carleton Watkins）以鏡頭記錄下優勝美地的壯麗景觀，照片傳遍全美，深深影響了當時的社會與政界。1864年，美國總統林肯簽署《優勝美地贈與法案》（Yosemite Grant），首次以聯邦法令永久保護這片山谷，為日後美國國家公園制度奠定了重要基礎。"
  en="Yosemite Falls is more than the most recognizable landmark in Yosemite Valley; it also occupies an important place in the history of American conservation. Long before Europeans entered the valley, the Ahwahneechee people regarded these mountains and waterfalls as sacred, believing the thunder of the falls to embody the living power of nature. In 1851, European explorers became the first outsiders to record their impressions of Yosemite Valley, where the immense waterfall left them awestruck. A decade later, the pioneering photographer Carleton Watkins captured Yosemite's grandeur in a series of remarkable photographs that circulated throughout the United States, profoundly influencing both the public and political leaders. In 1864, President Abraham Lincoln signed the Yosemite Grant, placing the valley under permanent protection—the first federal action of its kind and a milestone that helped lay the foundation for America's national park system."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="攀登優勝美地瀑布步道，是親近這座瀑布最經典的方式。這條步道往返約十一點六公里，海拔爬升超過八百米，雖然距離並不算長，卻因坡度陡峭，被列為公園內最具挑戰性的經典一日健行路線之一。起初道路穿行於松林之間，隨著高度漸增，視野逐漸開闊，整個優勝美地山谷盡收眼底。半圓頂、哨兵岩、默塞德河，以及遠處連綿起伏的內華達山脈，層層展開，如同一幅徐徐鋪陳的山水長卷。"
  en="The Yosemite Falls Trail offers the classic route to experience this magnificent waterfall. Although the round-trip distance is only about 11.6 kilometers, the climb exceeds 800 meters in elevation, making it one of Yosemite's most demanding and rewarding day hikes. The trail begins beneath towering pines before gradually opening onto expansive views of Yosemite Valley. Half Dome, Sentinel Rock, the Merced River, and the distant ridges of the Sierra Nevada unfold one after another like an immense landscape scroll painted across the horizon."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="越接近瀑布，轟鳴之聲越發震撼。春季水量最大時，飛濺的水霧可隨風飄灑數十米，即使站在步道之上，也常被細密的水珠打濕衣衫。那不是尋常瀑布的柔美，而是一種天地偉力的展現：萬噸雪水日夜奔流，將千萬年不變的花崗岩沖刷得愈加雄渾，令人深切感受到大自然的壯麗與永恆。"
  en="As one draws nearer, the roar of the waterfall becomes ever more overwhelming. During the height of spring runoff, clouds of spray drift dozens of meters on the wind, soaking hikers long before they reach the brink. This is not merely the graceful beauty of falling water, but a display of nature's immense power. Countless tons of snowmelt thunder day and night across granite that has endured for millions of years, creating a spectacle that inspires both awe and reverence."
%}

{% include xinghe/paragraph.html
  zh="然而，真正令人難忘的，並非山谷中仰望飛瀑，而是最終登上瀑布之巔。奔騰一路的溪流，在腳下忽然收束成一道平靜的優勝美地溪，清澈見底，緩緩流淌，彷彿絲毫不知前方就是七百多米的萬丈深淵。只需再向前數步，溪水便突然躍出懸崖，化作震撼世界的優勝美地瀑布。站在瀑布頂端俯瞰山谷，巨大的落差令人心生敬畏，而回望身後，溪流依舊安然流淌。"
  en="Yet the most unforgettable experience is not gazing upward from the valley below, but standing upon the very crest of the falls. After rushing down from the high country, Yosemite Creek suddenly gathers into a calm, crystal-clear stream at one's feet, flowing so gently that it seems unaware of the immense precipice only steps away. Then, without warning, the tranquil water leaps over the granite edge and becomes Yosemite Falls. Looking down from the brink, one cannot help but marvel at the immense drop into the valley below; turning back, however, the creek continues its peaceful course as though nothing extraordinary were about to happen."
%}
